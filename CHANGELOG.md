# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-26

- Plays better on phones: full screen on every phone, iPhone included, with a "Play full screen" button under the game.
- A phone held sideways gets its own layout: the whole keyboard fits beside a full-height ladder, and the result card puts your climb in its own column.
- Letter tiles and keys catch near misses, buttons are bigger on touch screens, and there is no long-press menu on the keyboard.
- A gentle buzz on each new rung, a double buzz for a word that does not fit, and a bigger one when you ring the bell (Android, with sound on).

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-25

- After each of today's ladders, tell us how hard it was with one tap, and see how your rungs compare with everyone else's today. Anonymous: nothing about you is stored.
- A new "Tell us" card under the game: a thumbs up or down and a few one-tap notes (too hard, too easy, broke on my phone...), no account needed.
- A Privacy link in the footer.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-24

First release.

- A word ladder with an odd little robot on it: change one letter per rung, every rung a real word, climb from the bottom word to the top one.
- **Daily:** three ladders a day (Short, Tall, Towering), the same for everyone, with streaks, a 14-day archive and a spoiler-free result to copy.
- **Journey:** 60 hand-picked ladders in 6 chapters, from CAT → DOG up to long five-letter climbs. Up to 3 stars each.
- **Endless:** random ladders at 3, 4 or 5 letters and three heights.
- Two ways to enter a word: tap a letter then a key to swap just that letter, or type the whole word. Built-in on-screen keyboard, plus the real one.
- No way to fail: undo, hints, and a "show me the robot's route" flag.
- The sky changes as you climb: treetops, clouds, dusk, stars. A bell and confetti at the top.
- Word list: the public-domain ENABLE list (any real 3-5 letter word is accepted), with puzzles built only from a hand-picked list of everyday words.
