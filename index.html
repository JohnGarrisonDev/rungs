Word puzzle

Rungs

One letter per rung. Climb from one word to another.

How to play

  • The bottom rung is the start word, the top rung is the goal. Every word you enter becomes the next rung, and the robot climbs.
  • Each rung must be a real word that changes exactly one letter of the rung below. COLD → CORD → CARD → WARD → WARM.
  • Letters that already match the goal turn green with an underline.
  • You cannot fail: undo steps back down, a hint gives the next word, and the ladder just grows if you wander. Fewer rungs is better. Par is the robot's own shortest route through everyday words.
  • Daily: three ladders a day, the same for everyone. Finish all three without giving up to keep your streak.
  • Journey: 60 ladders, 3 stars each. Endless: pick a word length and a height.

Controls

  • Quick way: tap a letter of the next rung, then tap the key to swap in. One letter changed, done.
  • Or type the whole word and press Enter. Backspace deletes.
  • ← → pick a letter to change · ↓ undo (climb down) · ? hint · Esc back
  • The on-screen keyboard works with mouse and touch, so phones never need their own keyboard. The buttons beside the next rung undo and hint; the flag gives up. On a phone, Play full screen gives the ladder the whole screen, upright or sideways.
